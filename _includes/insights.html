Emptiness Is an Abstract Class · 空即是色

Emptiness is an abstract class, Form a concrete class, Appearance an instance. You see a cat: this specific cat is the instance, "cat" is the abstract class, "black cat" the concrete class. The mind moves from concrete to abstract — a verifiable cognitive path.

Emptiness : Form : Appearance = abstract class : concrete class : instance
Diamond Sutra · Design

"I" Cannot Be Instantiated

"I" is fundamentally an abstract class: cannot use this → no self; cannot instantiate → no person; no lifecycle methods → no lifespan. The end of the four lines is an object that can never be new-ed.

no this · no instantiate · no lifecycle

More Knowledge, Less Ego · Ego = 1 / Knowledge

"More the knowledge, lesser the ego; lesser the knowledge, more the ego." And the caution that follows: "We should take care not to make the intellect our god — it has powerful muscles, but no personality."

Two swallows return to the southern lands,
Seeking the halls of old noble clans.
Spring noon is still in the painted room;
Here they will nest, and life will bloom.
Fortune returns through earth and sky;
Wealth gathers bright from eight quarters nigh.
Life’s road takes on a brighter hue;
